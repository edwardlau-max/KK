Incident

Poo Leaking Procedure

Evacuate the pool for Level 3 only
  1. Identify the critical level (see table).
  2. Level 1 or 2: clear the debris directly and isolate from pool.
  3. Level 3 only: evacuate the pool.
  4. Clear the debris with vacuum and net ASAP.
  5. Dose 100g CL after clearing all debris.
  6. Cancel class for 30 mins after dosing for pool buffer, then start class after that.
LevelWhat it means
Level 1Leaking on the high wall / big mat / pool deck (isolated from pool)
Level 2Solid poo which can be cleared immediately
Level 3Poo spread within a pool / more than a pool (fibre type)
Incident

Vomit Procedure

Inform admin and log every incident in the CRM

Type 1 Pool water, liquid or milk only (no food)

  1. Take the child out, check they are OK, clean and resume the lesson.
  2. Class continues as normal.
  3. Inform admin and log it in the CRM.

Type 2 Contains food or stomach contents

  1. Stop the class immediately and get that group of parents and children out of the pool or to another pool that is not affected.
  2. Coach spends 15 minutes clearing all the debris with the net.
  3. Parent with vomit on their skin must shower to clear debris before re-entering.
  4. Inform admin and log it in the CRM.
